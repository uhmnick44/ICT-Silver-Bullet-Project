# Contract-roll report — convention `lab_volume_crossover_next_session_v1`

This is the lab's own roll convention. The original research's vendor convention is unknown; alternative conventions can be registered in `engine/rolls.py::ROLL_METHODS` and selected with `[roll].method` for sensitivity testing.

## Rule

- Method: volume_crossover; confirmation_days = 1; forward_only = True; tie_break = incumbent
- Crossover on trading date D (challenger daily volume strictly greater than incumbent) takes effect on the next trading date D+1 (no look-ahead).
- Roll timestamp = first bar of the new contract in the effective session (nominal open 18:00 ET of the preceding calendar day).
- Offset = close(new) − close(old) at the 16:59 ET bar of the crossover session. Both contracts must have a valid bar at exactly that minute; otherwise the roll is flagged `needs_review`, no price is substituted and the pipeline aborts.
- Back-adjustment: additive, anchored to the latest contract (its adjustment is 0); bars before roll k receive the sum of offsets k..n.
- Initialisation: the first trading date uses its own session volume. This is NON-CAUSAL; that session is flagged `noncausal_contract_selection` and all its window-eligibility flags are False.

## Caveat on vendor roll conventions

The original Silver Bullet research used an unspecified vendor continuous contract. Vendor conventions (volume, open interest, fixed calendar day, Databento `.v.0`/`.n.0`/`.c.0`) differ from this rule by up to several trading days around each roll; several crossovers here are near-ties (see `near_tie_crossover`). Results from Layer 2+ must therefore be checked for sensitivity to the roll rule rather than assumed to match the vendor series.
